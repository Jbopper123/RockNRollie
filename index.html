How It Works

Attach the band to the stroller wheel, then rock the stroller gently back and forth on a flat indoor surface.

1

Wrap around the tire

Position the band around the tire so the raised bump sits on the tread.

2

Pull through to tighten

Pull the tail through the locking slot and tighten to the desired fit.

3

Tuck the tail

Slide the tail into the keeper loop for a secure hold.

4

Rock gently indoors

Use only while supervised, on a flat indoor surface, with gentle back-and-forth motion.

Safety note: RockNRollie is intended for supervised indoor rocking only. Do not use for outdoor walking, running, roads, slopes, stairs, kerbs, escalators, uneven ground, or unsupervised sleep. Remove before taking the stroller outside.
